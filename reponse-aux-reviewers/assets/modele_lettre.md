## Formules utiles (ton humble)

- We thank the reviewer for this careful check and apologise for the oversight.
- The reviewer is correct. On re-examination, we found that…
- We agree, and the sentence has been removed.
- This is a helpful point. [Ce qui a été fait.]
- We have clarified this in the Methods (lines A–B).

## À éviter

- « As we already explained… » (sec).
- « The reviewer misunderstood… » (contre-attaque).
- Toute promesse d'une modification qui n'est pas dans le manuscrit.
